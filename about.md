# About Me

Hi! I'm Sahil, an electrical engineering student at Purdue University and an aspiring jazz piano player. Over time I've developed an interest in all things robotics, especially dexterous robotic hands, path planning, and sensing and control. More broadly, I enjoy exploring different areas of engineering and learning new things simply because I'm curious. For anyone interested, I post piano covers of various jazz pieces on my instagram @sahil_piano_, go check it out.

This blog documents my interests, projects, and engineering journey.
